The project

What is Westin Residences Gurugram?

A Marriott-branded residential development in Sector 103 on the Dwarka Expressway, developed by Whiteland Corporation under Marriott International’s Westin brand. It offers 3 and 4 BHK homes across about 20 acres, planned around Westin’s well-being philosophy.

Who is the developer of Westin Residences Gurugram?

Whiteland Corporation Pvt. Ltd. is the promoter, and the project is registered as Urban Resort. Whiteland describes its portfolio as six marquee projects spanning 12 million sq ft.

How is Marriott involved?

The residences carry Marriott International’s Westin brand, and residents receive Marriott-managed services, with service and management standards drawn from Westin hospitality. Westin and ONVIA are brands of Marriott International, Inc.

What is ONVIA?

ONVIA is Marriott’s residence owner recognition platform. Ownership at Westin Residences Gurugram includes it, with preferred rates, VIP recognition and curated experiences at participating Marriott properties worldwide.

How large is the development?

About 20 acres, with 70% of the land left green and open, and a clubhouse of approximately 1.75 lakh sq ft. The towers are numbered 1 to 9, with Towers 4A and 4B alongside Tower 4.

Location

Where is Westin Residences Gurugram located?

Sector 103, on the Dwarka Expressway in Gurugram, Haryana.

How far is it from the airport?

About 15 minutes by road from Indira Gandhi International Airport.

What is near Sector 103?

Drive times from the site: Global City 5 minutes, Trumpet Flyover 7 minutes, Yashobhoomi (IICC) 10 minutes, Bharat Vandana Park 12 minutes, Dwarka Golf Course 13 minutes, and Diplomatic Enclave II 15 minutes.

Why is the Dwarka Expressway location significant?

The Dwarka Expressway is the 29-kilometre corridor connecting Dwarka in Delhi to the Kherki Daula toll on NH-48. It puts Sector 103 within a short drive of the airport, Yashobhoomi (IICC) and Diplomatic Enclave II.

Homes and floor plans

What configurations are available?

3 and 4 BHK residences in five layouts, from 2,673 to 4,328 sq ft saleable area. Every layout is set out on the floor plan page.

What are the 3 BHK sizes?

The 3 BHK Small is 2,673 sq ft saleable with 1,431 sq ft carpet and a 431 sq ft balcony. The 3 BHK Large is 2,939 sq ft saleable with 1,577 sq ft carpet and a 482 sq ft balcony.

What are the 4 BHK sizes?

The 4 BHK Small is 3,750 sq ft saleable with 1,969 sq ft carpet and a 318 sq ft balcony. The 4 BHK Medium is 3,987 sq ft with 2,095 sq ft carpet and a 376 sq ft balcony. The 4 BHK Large is 4,328 sq ft with 2,211 sq ft carpet and a 585 sq ft balcony.

Which towers have which apartments?

The 3 BHK Small is in Towers 1, 2, 3, 4, 4A and 4B. The 3 BHK Large is in Towers 2, 3, 4 and 4A. The 4 BHK Small is in Towers 7, 8 and 9. The 4 BHK Medium and 4 BHK Large are in Towers 5 and 6.

What specifications do the apartments have?

Imported Italian marble, VRV air conditioning with air-purifier provision, smart-home automation, a full modular kitchen with Bosch or Siemens appliances, V&B, Grohe and TOTO bathrooms, eight high-speed elevators per tower, and 100% DG power backup.

Amenities

What is in the clubhouse?

The clubhouse is approximately 1.75 lakh sq ft. It includes a three-meals restaurant, multi-cuisine restaurants, private dining rooms, a casual snack café, bars and a juice bar, BBQ areas, a ballroom for 300 guests, a 25-seat movie theatre, billiards and game rooms, a library and residents’ lounge, a kids’ club, and guest suites with concierge.

What wellness facilities are there?

A Westin spa with treatment rooms, a dedicated Ayurveda room, a yoga studio and deck, an all-weather indoor pool, sauna and steam with jacuzzi, and a juice bar and salon.

What sports facilities are there?

A gymnasium, tennis and badminton courts, squash and pickleball courts, a half-court basketball court, outdoor pools for adults and children, and outdoor running tracks.

Is there vehicle movement at ground level?

No. The master plan keeps vehicles off the ground level. The towers stand on 20 to 30 ft stilts above grand ground-floor lobbies, and cycling, walking and jogging tracks run around the perimeter.
