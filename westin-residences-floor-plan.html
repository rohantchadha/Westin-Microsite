Reading Carpet Against Saleable Area

Saleable area is what the price is quoted against. Carpet area is the usable floor inside the walls, and it is the number RERA requires a developer to declare. At Westin Residences the ratio sits between roughly 51 and 54 per cent, which is typical for branded residences of this tier, where lobbies, the 1.75 lakh sq ft clubhouse and the wide tower spacing are carried in the loading.

The balcony is listed separately here because it varies sharply between layouts — from 318 sq ft on the 4 BHK Small to 585 sq ft on the 4 BHK Large. For a home on the Dwarka Expressway, where the towers stand on 20 to 30 foot stilts with clear views across the central gardens, that difference is worth weighing carefully.

The number that matters is not the one on the price list. It is the carpet area, the balcony, and the tower the apartment sits in. Absolute Realty · Authorised Channel Partner

Floor Plan Questions

How many floor plans does Westin Residences Gurugram offer?

Five. Two 3 BHK layouts at 2,673 and 2,939 sq ft, and three 4 BHK layouts at 3,750, 3,987 and 4,328 sq ft saleable area. Each is allocated to specific towers within the nine-tower development.

What is the carpet area of the Westin Residences 3 BHK?

The 3 BHK Small has a carpet area of 1,431 sq ft against 2,673 sq ft saleable, with a 431 sq ft balcony. The 3 BHK Large has 1,577 sq ft carpet against 2,939 sq ft saleable, with a 482 sq ft balcony.

What is the largest apartment at Westin Residences?

The 4 BHK Large at 4,328 sq ft saleable area, with 2,211 sq ft carpet and a 585 sq ft balcony. It is available in Towers 5 and 6.

Which towers have 4 BHK apartments?

Towers 5 and 6 carry the 4 BHK Medium and 4 BHK Large layouts. Towers 7, 8 and 9 carry the 4 BHK Small. The 3 BHK layouts sit in Towers 1, 2, 3, 4, 4A and 4B.
