What the Marriott name changes

Westin and ONVIA are brands of Marriott International. The branding governs how the residential services are specified and run rather than the construction itself, which is Whiteland Corporation's. In practice that is what separates a serviced amenity from an unstaffed one — a concierge desk that is attended, a spa with trained therapists, restaurants that operate on a schedule rather than opening when a contractor is found.

Amenity Questions

More answers on location, homes, payment and RERA are in the Westin Residences FAQ.

How big is the clubhouse at Westin Residences Gurugram?

Approximately 1.75 lakh sq ft. It holds the wellness block, the dining formats, the indoor pool, the ballroom and the residents' lounge and library, alongside the games and kids' spaces.

Does Westin Residences have a swimming pool?

It has three: an all-weather indoor pool inside the wellness block, and separate outdoor adult and children's pools. The poolside bar sits alongside the outdoor deck.

What sports facilities are there at Westin Residences?

Tennis, badminton, squash, pickleball and half-court basketball, plus a gymnasium and outdoor running tracks. Squash and pickleball courts are uncommon in Gurugram residential developments at this scale.

Is there a spa at Westin Residences Gurugram?

Yes. The wellness block has spa treatment rooms, a dedicated Ayurveda room, a yoga studio with an outdoor deck, sauna and steam with a jacuzzi, and a salon with a juice bar.

Can residents host events at Westin Residences?

The clubhouse includes a ballroom for around 300 guests, private dining rooms and a 25-seat movie theatre. Guest suites are available for visiting family.

How much of the site is open space?

Around 70% of the 20-acre development is green and open space, with the nine towers arranged around landscaped grounds. Outdoor amenities — pools, courts, running tracks and barbecue areas — sit within that.
