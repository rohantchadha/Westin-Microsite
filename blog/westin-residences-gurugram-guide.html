What is Westin Residences Gurgaon?

Westin Residences Gurgaon is a branded residential development in which the day-to-day expression of the home — arrival, wellness, service culture, community — is shaped by Marriott International's Westin hospitality brand. It is not a hotel with apartments above it. It is a private residential address that draws on the operating philosophy of one of the world's most recognised well-being brands.

For a buyer scanning Gurugram's luxury market for a home that carries an international provenance, this is one of a small handful of options — and the largest of them by scale.

Location: Sector 103, on the Dwarka Expressway

Westin Residences Sector 103 sits on the Dwarka Expressway — the 29-kilometre corridor connecting Dwarka in Delhi to the Kherki Daula toll on NH-48. The address places the community within a straightforward drive of Indira Gandhi International Airport (approximately 15 kilometres), Cyber City and Golf Course Road (via the Southern Peripheral Road and NH-48), and the emerging commercial hubs along the expressway itself.

The Dwarka Expressway address matters for two reasons. The first is the obvious one — connectivity to the airport and to the older Gurugram business districts. The second is quieter but arguably more consequential: this stretch is where the next generation of Gurugram's luxury inventory is being planted, and the sector has attracted a cluster of grade-A residential launches alongside Westin Residences Dwarka Expressway.

Amenities and Everyday Life

The residence experience is anchored by an expansive clubhouse of approximately 1.75 lakh sq. ft. — one of the larger amenity footprints on the Dwarka Expressway — organised around wellness, recreation and dining. The towers themselves are elevated on 20 to 30 foot stilts, giving the ground plane over to biophilic landscape rather than parking.

Highlights across the community include:

  • A signature outdoor pool with landscaped deck
  • The Westin Spa and treatment rooms, drawing on the brand's wellness philosophy
  • A fitness centre with dedicated studios for yoga, Pilates and functional training
  • Fine-dining and all-day café spaces within the clubhouse
  • Private residents' lounges and co-working suites
  • Children's play zones, indoor and outdoor
  • Concierge and Marriott-managed services for residents

Marriott Branded Residences in India — A Short Primer

Marriott branded residences in India remain a small but expanding category. What separates a branded residence from a conventional luxury apartment is that the operating brand — in this case, Westin — is contractually involved in how the residential experience is delivered: arrival, service culture, wellness programming and community rituals.

Residence ownership at Westin Residences Gurugram includes enrolment in ONVIA, Marriott's residence owner recognition platform — a global programme with preferred rates, VIP recognition and curated experiences at participating Marriott properties worldwide. For a household that already travels internationally on Marriott, this rounds out the ownership case.
